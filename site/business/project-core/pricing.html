FAQ

Can I use V-Universe in my commercial product?

Yes — under either license. AGPL requires source disclosure; commercial does not.

What counts as a "derivative work"?

Calling bwrap-enhanced.sh as an external command is generally not derivative. Modifying source code triggers AGPL.

Do I need a CLA to contribute?

Yes. CLA grants Frederick Bloom / Hanaden dual-licensing rights without transferring copyright.

Is there a trial?

The open source version is fully functional. Evaluate under AGPL, then contact for commercial terms.
