Anti-Fixation Guards

Every node in the SDLC hierarchy has guards that fire on every state transition. These guards prevent:

Tunnel Vision

Focusing too narrowly on one solution when better alternatives exist. Guards force exploration of the solution space.

Stale Assumptions

Continuing to build on assumptions that are no longer valid. Guards periodically re-validate premises.

Cargo Cult

Doing things because "we've always done it this way." Guards challenge conventions and demand justification.

Completion Theater

Reporting success based on activity metrics. Guards verify actual outcomes, not just process compliance.

Emergency Breakers

When guards fail and the system enters a death spiral — escalating pressure, narrowing focus, reactive fixes — emergency breakers trip:

  • Hard constraints: Resource limits, time limits, scope limits that cannot be overridden
  • Asymmetric data: Deliberately introducing outside perspectives and conflicting information
  • Chaos engineering: Randomly disabling components to find hidden dependencies
  • Governance review: Human oversight when automated guards are insufficient

After a breaker trips, the system undergoes a full reset: realign strategy, capture lessons learned, and re-arm guards with stronger rules.
