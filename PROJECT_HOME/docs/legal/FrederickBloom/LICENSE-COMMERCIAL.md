## Overview

The Hanaden Project (`bwrap-enhanced` and related subsystems) is made available under a dual-licensing business model:
1. **Open Source (AGPL-3.0-only):** Free for open-source and reciprocal copyleft use.
2. **Commercial License:** For proprietary embedding, SaaS/hosted services, closed-source products, enterprise internal deployment without copyleft, or custom integration.

---

## When You Need a Commercial License

A separate commercial license is required if your organization intends to:

- **Embed in Closed-Source Products:** Distribute `bwrap-enhanced` or derivative software within proprietary tools or binary distributions without releasing corresponding source code.
- **Hosted Cloud / SaaS Deployments:** Run `bwrap-enhanced` as an execution/sandbox backend accessible over a network without providing AGPL-3.0 source access to remote users.
- **Internal Enterprise Use Without Copyleft:** Modify `bwrap-enhanced` for internal operations while keeping organizational customizations private.
- **AI & Model Training:** Train, fine-tune, evaluate, or benchmark machine learning models on Hanaden code, architectures, or designs.
- **OEM & White-Label Redistribution:** Rebrand or distribute Hanaden sandbox components under private-label or hardware bundle agreements.

## How to Obtain a Commercial License

To initiate a commercial licensing inquiry:

1. Email: **devlabs@hanaden.com**
2. Subject: `Commercial License Inquiry — [Your Organization Name]`
3. Include:
   - Organization name and jurisdiction
   - Target product/service description
   - Projected deployment volume / user base
   - Desired licensing tier
