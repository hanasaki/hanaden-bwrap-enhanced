# FullCoverageMandate: 100% Spec Constraint Coverage Mandatory

## Specification Statement

Every constraint declared in a specification document MUST be validated by
at least one test. A specification with untested constraints is considered
incomplete. The tdd.state MUST NOT advance to GREEN or PASS until all
constraints have corresponding passing tests.

## Rationale

Partial coverage creates a false sense of security. If 8 of 10 constraints
are tested, the 2 untested constraints may harbor defects that only surface
in production.

## Constraints

| Constraint | Value | Condition |
|------------|-------|-----------|
| 100% constraint coverage required | All constraints tested | Before GREEN |
| Untested constraint blocks GREEN | tdd.state stuck at RED | Always |
